# "Provably optimal"

- The docs call the dissemination strategy provably optimal for throughput and latency. ([docs intro](https://docs.getoptimum.xyz/docs/learn/overview/intro)) [claimed]
- The same text limits this to standard RLNC models, under loss and contention. Outside those models the proof does not automatically apply. ([docs intro](https://docs.getoptimum.xyz/docs/learn/overview/intro)) [confirmed]
- The trilemma blog bases the optimal-throughput claim on existing research, citing Deb, Médard and Choute (IEEE Trans. Inf. Theory 52(6), 2006). It is not a new proof. ([trilemma blog, 2025-07-30](https://www.getoptimum.xyz/blog/unlocking-the-blockchain-trilemma)) [confirmed]
- The research list includes "Algebraic Gossip: A Network Coding Approach to Optimal Multiple Rumor Mongering" (Allerton 2004, journal 2006). ([research list](https://docs.getoptimum.xyz/docs/research/gossip/gossip)) [confirmed]
- In the March 2026 RLNC vs Reed-Solomon video, Optimum's research engineer scopes the claim: RLNC is mathematically optimal for recovering erasures in networks, not for every coding problem. ([RLNC vs Reed-Solomon video, 2026-03-25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=M1RCYMWJguo)) [claimed]

## Bigger claims built on it

- The trilemma blog claims RLNC gossip scales without giving up decentralization or security. ([trilemma blog](https://www.getoptimum.xyz/blog/unlocking-the-blockchain-trilemma)) [claimed]
- It also claims this enables larger blocks and shorter slot times. ([same](https://www.getoptimum.xyz/blog/unlocking-the-blockchain-trilemma)) [claimed]

## How to write about it

"Provably optimal under standard RLNC models" is accurate. "Provably optimal" alone drops the condition.
