# Research history

## Key papers

- Koetter and Médard, "Beyond Routing: An Algebraic Approach to Network Coding", IEEE INFOCOM 2002. ([PDF on MIT site](https://www.mit.edu/~medard/01019253.pdf)) [confirmed]
- Ho, Médard, Koetter, Karger, Effros, Shi, Leong, "A Random Linear Network Coding Approach to Multicast", IEEE Transactions on Information Theory 52(10), October 2006, pp. 4413-4430. ([Caltech record](https://authors.library.caltech.edu/records/e9xap-44498)) [confirmed]
- Deb, Médard, Choute, "Algebraic Gossip", IEEE Transactions on Information Theory 52(6), 2006, earlier at Allerton 2004. Optimum cites it as the basis for its throughput claims. ([trilemma blog](https://www.getoptimum.xyz/blog/unlocking-the-blockchain-trilemma), [research list](https://docs.getoptimum.xyz/docs/research/gossip/gossip)) [confirmed]

## Optimum's own timeline

- Network coding 2000, RLNC invented at MIT 2003, landmark IEEE paper 2006. ([team page](https://www.getoptimum.xyz/team)) [claimed]
- Docs say RLNC was developed under Professor Médard's supervision. ([docs intro](https://docs.getoptimum.xyz/docs/learn/overview/intro)) [claimed]

## Commercial history before Optimum

- Code On Technologies spun RLNC out of Caltech and MIT and licenses it as a proprietary algorithm (reported 2014). ([Fierce Network, 2014-06-08](https://www.fierce-network.com/tech/code-licensing-its-technology-which-uses-algebra-to-speed-up-networks)) [third-party]
- Code On lists Médard as an RLNC co-inventor. ([Code On](https://www.codeontechnologies.com/company/about)) [third-party]

## Awards

- 2022 IEEE Koji Kobayashi Computers and Communications Award, to Médard personally, for network coding and optical and wireless work. The launch blog phrases it as if RLNC itself was honored; the team page makes clear it was her award. ([team page](https://www.getoptimum.xyz/team)) [claimed]
- 2026 IEEE Richard W. Hamming Medal, to Médard, per Optimum. Not independently checked yet. ([team page](https://www.getoptimum.xyz/team)) [claimed]

## Disputed

- **When and where RLNC was invented.** Optimum says invented at MIT in 2003. Fierce says invented in 2003 across several universities including Caltech. The earliest Koetter-Médard paper found is from INFOCOM 2002, and the 2006 multicast paper lists authors from both MIT and Caltech. Safe wording: developed by researchers at MIT and Caltech in the early 2000s, with Médard as a co-inventor.
