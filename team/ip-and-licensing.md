# IP and licensing

## What is known

- Optimum's launch blog has a heading calling RLNC an "MIT-Incubated Breakthrough". It describes RLNC, not Optimum as a company. ([launch blog](https://www.getoptimum.xyz/blog/introducing-optimum-building-the-missing-memory-layer-for-web3)) [claimed]
- No patent or license terms are published in the launch blog or docs. [confirmed: absence]
- Code On Technologies, a separate company, licenses RLNC as proprietary technology spun out of Caltech and MIT. ([Fierce, 2014](https://www.fierce-network.com/tech/code-licensing-its-technology-which-uses-algebra-to-speed-up-networks)) [third-party]
