# Funding

## Seed round, April 2025

- Optimum closed an $11M seed round led by 1kx. ([launch blog, 2025-04-15](https://www.getoptimum.xyz/blog/introducing-optimum-building-the-missing-memory-layer-for-web3)) [confirmed]
- The Defiant reported the same $11M seed led by 1kx. ([The Defiant, 2025-04-15](https://thedefiant.io/news/blockchains/optimum-secures-11m-seed-funding-led-1kx-decentralized-blockchain-memory-layer-d0b2a949)) [third-party]
- The round was announced at Consensus Toronto 2025. ([team page](https://www.getoptimum.xyz/team)) [claimed]

### Participating funds

Robot Ventures, CMT Digital, Spartan, Finality Capital, SNZ, Triton Capital, Big Brain, CMS, LongHash, NGC, Animoca, GSR, Caladan, Reforge. ([launch blog](https://www.getoptimum.xyz/blog/introducing-optimum-building-the-missing-memory-layer-for-web3)) [claimed]

### Angels (partial list)

The team page lists 22 angels, including Sandeep Nailwal (Polygon), Gracy Chen (Bitget), Rand Hindi (Zama), Tal Tchwella (Solana) and Saurabh Sharma (Jump Crypto). ([team page](https://www.getoptimum.xyz/team)) [claimed]
